Inspired by Geometry Wars, Gridform is the largest solo project I’ve worked on. The game contains over 75 uniquely designed levels filled with over-stimulating visuals.

The project was started mid 2020 and took about 2 years of development before being published in July 2022. Created in Unity.

[Available on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1608710/Gridform/)
